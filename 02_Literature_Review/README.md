# 02 - Literature Review Synthesis

| Theme | Supporting Studies | Common Dataset / Data Source | Methods / Approaches | Key Findings / Trends | Limitations / Research Gap |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Theme 1: Machine Learning and Tree-Based NIDS** | Qazi et al. (2024), Altulaihan et al. (2024), Zhang (2022), Sadhwani et al. (2023), Alazab et al. (2024) | CICIDS2018, IoTID20, KDD CUP99, TON-IoT, NSL-KDD | Random Forest, Decision Trees, Naïve Bayes, Support Vector Machines, Ensemble ML | Traditional ML classifiers achieve baseline accuracies with low training computational overhead. | Vulnerable to rare attack classes, class imbalance, and performance drops on modern traffic. |
| **Theme 2: Deep Learning and Hybrid Architectures** | He et al. (2024), Hnamte and Hussain (2023), Hairab et al. (2023), Zhang (2024), Alsulami (2024), Strickland et al. (2024) | CICIDS2017, UNSW-NB15, Edge-IIoT, CICIDS2019 | CNN-LSTM, Vision Transformers (De-ViT), DRL-GAN, Autoencoders, Multi-Attention Transformers | Hybrid deep learning models effectively capture complex spatial temporal patterns achieving high accuracy rates. | Intensive resource requirements and longer inference times. |
| **Theme 3: IoT and Industrial IoT Security** | Kareem (2025), Ferrão et al. (2023), Bakhsh et al. (2023), Wang et al. (2023), Deng (2024) | Bot-IoT, CIC-IoT22, Edge-IIoT, custom IoT logs | LightGBM, CGAN, MBConv-ViT, One-Class Autoencoders, Lightweight Ensemble Models | Specialized models successfully protect low powered IoT and smart industry environments against targeted attacks. | Limited to specific IoT protocol architectures |
| **Theme 4: Optimization and Feature Reduction Strategies** | Wang et al. (2023), Cohen et al. (2023), Zhang et al. (2024), Huang et al. (2024) | KDD99, Custom Traffic Logs, UNSW-NB15, CICIDS2017 | Feature Selection, Lightweight Ensembling, Dimensionality Reduction, Test-Time Data Augmentation | Feature engineering and model optimization significantly reduce computational overhead while maintaining high detection accuracy | High sensitivity to hyperparameter tuning and reliance on clean feature sets prior to deployment |
